📖 How to Play

The Basics

  • The game is designed for a teacher-led classroom. Teams do not need devices; the teacher controls the board.
  • Teams start off the board. On their turn, the teacher rolls the dice, and the team's counter lands on the corresponding square.
  • Every square requires the team to answer a language question. Correct answers award points and moves; incorrect answers result in penalties.

Special Squares

  • Normal Squares: Correct answer moves the team forward 1 square. Incorrect moves them back 1 square.
  • 🪜 Ladders: Answer correctly to unlock the Bonus Spinner and climb the ladder! Answer incorrectly, and you miss the opportunity and stay where you are.
  • 🐍 Snakes: Answer correctly to safely avoid the trap and stay where you are. Answer incorrectly, and you fall into the pit—spin the Bonus Spinner to see how far you drop!
  • ⚾ Curveballs: A high-risk, high-reward square. Answer correctly for a chance at massive points or swapping positions with the leader. Answer incorrectly and risk losing points or falling to the back of the pack. (Curveballs also apply a standard +/- 1 move).

The Golden Rule of Movement

Teams only follow the instructions of the square their dice roll initially places them on.

If a team moves forward or backwards as a result of answering a question (the +/- 1 rule, or via the Bonus Spinner), they DO NOT trigger the event of the new square they land on. The turn simply ends.

Winning the Game

To win the race, a team must reach the final "Finish" square and correctly answer the final question. If they get it wrong, they fall back 6 spaces!

There are two winners recognised at the end: The team that finishes the race first, and the team with the highest accumulated score.
