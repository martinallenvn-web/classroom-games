How to Play Kaboom!

Overview: Kaboom is a teacher-hosted game. Display the screen on your projector. Teams take turns answering. A correct answer earns Dollars and a "Kerching!". A wrong answer results in a "Kaboom!".

Question Types

  • Standard / True-False: Simply click the correct option. It evaluates immediately. True/False questions may include explanatory pop-ups upon evaluation.
  • Any Correct: Multiple options might be right, but the team only needs to click ONE of them. Evaluates immediately.
  • Multi-Select: Click 2 or more options to highlight them, then click "Submit Answer". ALL correct answers must be selected to win.
  • Ordering: Click options in the correct sequence. Numbers will appear to show your order. Click "Submit Answer" to evaluate.

Setting Up Teams

On the Game page, use the + / - buttons in the sidebar to adjust the number of teams (up to 6). Click directly on a team's name to edit it.
