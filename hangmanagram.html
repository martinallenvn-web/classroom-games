How to Play HANGMANAGRAM

Phase 1: Accumulation

  • The Question Gate: Teams must correctly answer a question to unlock the board. Correct = +10 pts.
  • Question Looping: If all questions have been exhausted, the same set of questions will automatically be used again to enable play to continue.
  • Letter Selection: Pick a letter.
    • High Freq (incl. S): 5 pts per instance.
    • Mid Freq: 20 pts per instance.
    • Low Freq: 30 pts per instance.
  • Penalties: The turn automatically passes to the next team after every guess. Missing a letter incurs a scaled life penalty depending on the number of teams playing.
  • Skip Phase: A button is available in the top menu to end Phase 1 early and skip to the Anagram.

Pleading for Mercy (Phase 1)

If a team loses their last life in Phase 1, they get an automatic spin to plead for mercy. They may only successfully save one life per turn this way, but if they survive and lose their life again on a later turn, they can plead again.

  • 3 in 6 Chance: The executioner spares a limb (+1 life restored).
  • 2 in 6 Chance: The executioner is undecided. Spin again!
  • 1 in 6 Chance: No Mercy! The team is frozen.

The Corrupt Executioner's Market (Phase 1.5)

The Market opens when all teams are frozen OR Phase 1 completes. Frozen teams can buy limbs back.

  • Base Cost: Starts at 5 points per team. Teams can only save one limb per market visit!
  • Similar odds to Pleading, but Greed (2/6 chance) means gold is pocketed and no life is restored!
  • If a team lacks the points to spin, their fate lies at the whim of the Host (Executioner), who may manually adjust scores or restore lives.

Phase 2: Positioning (The Endgame)

Once all letters have been extracted, the Clue and Grid are revealed.

  • Phase 2 Questions: Answer correctly (+10 points) to pick a letter. If answered wrongly, you may pay a 10 pt penalty to Spin for Mercy or lose a limb.
  • The Timer: After placing a letter, the 10-second countdown starts.
  • Stopping the Clock: If the team shouts an answer, click "Stop the Clock" to verify it. If incorrect, the Mercy Spin penalty triggers again!
  • Victory: A correct guess awards a 30-point bonus and reveals the final Podium!
