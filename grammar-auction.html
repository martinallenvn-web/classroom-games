How to Play Grammar Auction

  • Objective: Teams start with a pot of cash ($1000). The goal is to finish with the most money.
  • The Auction: A sentence is displayed on the board. Teams discuss whether it is Correct or Incorrect.
  • Betting: Teams secretly write down their guess and bet amount.
    • Minimum bet is $10.
    • Maximum bet is $50 (until the final question, where limits are lifted!).
  • The Reveal & Payouts:
    • If the sentence is Correct: Teams who guessed Correct win their bet. Teams who guessed Incorrect lose it.
    • If the sentence is Incorrect:
      • Teams who guessed Incorrect win their stake instantly. They can double their winnings if they successfully write the correct sentence.
      • Teams who guessed Correct have their stake deducted and frozen. If they successfully write the correct sentence, their money is rescued (returned). If they fail, it remains lost.
  • Banker: The host can use the "+$50" button to bail out teams that hit $0.
